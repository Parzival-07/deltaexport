About us

Rooted in Indian trade, built for global buyers.

Delta Export began as a spice exporter and has grown into metal scrap and real estate, with the same focus on quality and honest dealing.

Who we are

A trusted partner in Indian spice trade.

Delta Export supplies high-quality Indian spices to markets around the world. We buy directly from farms we know across India, which lets us guarantee authenticity, purity and consistent quality.

Over the years we've added graded metal scrap and property brokerage. Each business runs on the same principles: clear specifications, honest pricing and dependable follow-through.
